# Cools Package-Substrate-Less SystemBoard

## Public Patent Portfolio Map

This document presents the public architecture of the Cools transferred-RDL motherboard and thermally active system-backbone patent portfolio.

The portfolio is structured as a connected system. Individual inventions protect the core package-substrate-less architecture, transfer manufacturing routes, yield and rework mechanisms, thermo-mechanical reliability, detectable structural fingerprints, thermal-backbone integration and electrical–optical extension.

This is a public summary only. Detailed specifications, claim language, drawings, process windows and non-public know-how are not provided here.

---

# A. Core Package-Substrate-Less Architecture

## A1. Temporary-carrier-transferred high-density redistribution body

**Korean title**  
임시 캐리어 전사식 고밀도 재배선체를 구비한 패키지기판 제거형 반도체 패키지 및 그 형성방법

**English title**  
Package-Substrateless Semiconductor Package Having a Temporary-Carrier-Transferred High-Density Redistribution Body and Method of Forming the Same

**Public scope**

- A high-density redistribution body is fabricated on a separate flat carrier.
- The body is released and directly joined to a coarse-pitch final system board.
- No additional organic package substrate is interposed between the redistribution body and the motherboard.
- The redistribution body can provide fine-pitch upper terminals, coarse-pitch lower terminals and internal fan-out conversion.

---

## A2. Local transferred fine-RDL patch

**Korean title**  
전사형 국부 미세 재배선 패치를 구비한 반도체 패키지 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Semiconductor Package Having a Transferred Local Fine Redistribution Patch and Method of Manufacturing the Same

**Public scope**

- Fine routing is localized beneath selected die-to-die or die-to-memory regions.
- The coarse system board does not need the fine pitch of the local patch.
- The patch is directly joined to the final system board without an intermediate package substrate.
- The patch can be independently manufactured, tested and replaced.

---

## A3. Board-scale transferred redistribution skin

**Korean title**  
조대 마더보드 상에 전사되는 보드스케일 고밀도 재배선 오버레이 및 이를 포함하는 재배선 일체형 시스템보드

**English title**  
Board-Scale Transferable High-Density Redistribution Overlay for Coarse Motherboard Integration and Redistribution-Integrated System Board Including the Same

**Public scope**

- A board-scale RDL overlay covers a large area, substantially the entire area or multiple continuous functional regions of a coarse motherboard.
- Fine-line routing is not fabricated directly in the motherboard.
- A separately manufactured RDL skin supplies the high-density redistribution function.
- The architecture can include a single sheet or multiple stitched sheets.

---

## A4. General overlay-transfer and connection platform

**Korean title**  
조대(粗大)한 배선 보드 상에 별도 제조된 고밀도 배선 오버레이를 전사·접속하는 방법 및 이를 포함하는 RDL 일체형 시스템보드

**English title**  
Method for Transferring and Connecting a Separately Fabricated High-Density Wiring Overlay onto a Coarse Wiring Board and RDL-Integrated System Board Including the Same

**Public scope**

- Covers local patches, large sheets, full-area overlays, segments and tile combinations.
- Includes pitch-conversion regions, alignment-error absorption, known-good inspection and repairable overlay concepts.
- Integrates the transferred overlay with coarse board wiring, vias, power planes, ground planes and external connections.

---

## A5. Package-substrate-function-integrated motherboard

**Korean title**  
패키지 기판 기능 통합형 보드레벨 재배선 마더보드, 이를 포함하는 반도체 모듈 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Board-Level Redistribution Motherboard Integrating Package-Substrate Functions, Semiconductor Module Including the Same, and Method for Manufacturing the Same

**Public scope**

- Transfers package-substrate fan-out, signal-distribution and power-distribution functions to the motherboard surface.
- Reduces or removes high-current plated through-via requirements in brittle cores.
- Includes high-voltage distribution, die-adjacent voltage conversion, tile-type RDL, local bridges and separated thermal paths.
- Allows complete via removal, partial via reduction or replacement by solid metal inlays and alternative vertical connections.

# B. Transfer Manufacturing Platforms

## B1. Semi-additive process on a flat temporary carrier

**Korean title**  
평탄 임시 캐리어 상의 세미애디티브 공정으로 형성한 미세배선 구조체를 시스템 실장용 보드에 직접 전사하는 패키지기판 제거형 반도체 패키지의 제조방법 및 그 미세배선 구조체

**English title**  
Method of Manufacturing a Package-Substrateless Semiconductor Package by Transferring a Fine-Wiring Structure Formed by a Semi-Additive Process on a Flat Temporary Carrier Directly to a System-Mounting Board, and the Fine-Wiring Structure

**Public scope**

- Uses existing PCB/package-substrate semi-additive processing equipment.
- Moves fine patterning from a rough and warped board to a flat carrier.
- Releases, inspects and directly transfers the completed fine-wiring body.
- Preserves the existing wiring-manufacturer process and supply chain.

---

## B2. Ultra-thin-copper-foil carrier without vacuum deposition

**Korean title**  
극박 동박 부착 캐리어 상에서 진공 증착 없이 형성되어 시스템 실장용 보드에 직접 전사되는 패키지기판 제거형 미세배선 구조체 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Package-Substrateless Fine-Wiring Structure Formed without Vacuum Deposition on an Ultra-Thin-Copper-Foil-Attached Carrier and Directly Transferred onto a System-Mounting Board, and Manufacturing Method Thereof

**Public scope**

- Uses ultra-thin copper foil as the electroplating feed layer.
- Eliminates carrier-side physical-vapor-deposited seed formation.
- Uses wet processing, lamination and exposure-based manufacturing.
- Produces embedded lower terminals, recessed terminal features or residual-copper fingerprints after foil etching.

---

## B3. Reusable carrier with protected reference surface

**Korean title**  
영구 보호층과 소모성 릴리스층을 구비한 재사용형 캐리어 어셈블리 및 이를 이용한 미세배선 구조체의 반복 제조방법

**English title**  
Reusable Carrier Assembly Having a Permanent Protective Layer and a Consumable Release Layer, and Method of Repeatedly Manufacturing Fine-Wiring Structures Using the Same

**Public scope**

- A permanent protective layer preserves the carrier reference surface.
- A consumable release layer is renewed for each manufacturing cycle.
- The carrier is cleaned, inspected and returned to production.
- Carrier cost is distributed across repeated fine-wiring manufacturing cycles.

---

## B4. Glass RDL and through-glass-via interface metallization

**Korean title**  
유리 기판 재배선층 및 유리관통전극의 금속화 계면 구조와 그 형성 방법

**English title**  
Metallization Interface Structure for Glass-Substrate Redistribution Layer and Through-Glass Via, and Method for Forming the Same

**Public scope**

- Separates glass adhesion, metal nucleation direction, metal-ion capture, copper diffusion blocking and low-resistance routing into functional layers.
- Includes an adhesion-anchor layer, nitrogen-containing metal-growth-directing layer, barrier layer and copper conductor.
- Supports wet, conformal metallization of planar glass and through-glass-via sidewalls.
- Includes measurable nitrogen and catalyst-metal concentration fingerprints.

# C. Yield, Repair and Heterogeneous Fan-Out

## C1. Known-good tile array and inter-patch bridge RDL

**Korean title**  
복수의 기지양품 재배선 타일과 패치간 브리지 재배선으로 구성되어 대면적 수율을 확보하는 재배선 오버레이 구조 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Redistribution Overlay Structure Composed of a Plurality of Known-Good Redistribution Tiles and Inter-Patch Bridge Redistribution to Secure Large-Area Yield, and Method of Manufacturing the Same

**Public scope**

- Separately manufactured and inspected known-good RDL tiles are arranged on a target surface.
- Inter-patch bridge redistribution connects adjacent tile edge pads.
- The tiles form one continuous or functionally continuous redistribution network.
- A defective tile can be individually removed and replaced.

## C2. Multi-zone fan-out with different conversion ratios

**Korean title**  
복수의 변환 구역이 서로 다른 변환비를 갖는 전사형 다구역 팬아웃 미세배선 구조체 및 그 반도체 패키지

**English title**  
Transfer-Type Multi-Zone Fan-Out Fine-Wiring Structure in Which a Plurality of Conversion Zones Have Different Conversion Ratios, and Semiconductor Package Having the Same

**Public scope**

- A single transferred body contains multiple planar conversion zones.
- Each zone can convert a different upper die pitch to a lower board pitch.
- Logic, memory, power-management and photonic devices can be supported by different routing geometries in one body.
- Direct die-to-die wiring can cross between selected zones without passing through the system board.

# D. Thermo-Mechanical Reliability and Detectability

**Korean title**  
전사된 미세배선 구조체와 시스템 실장용 보드 사이의 열기계 응력을 완충하는 접합부 구조를 갖는 반도체 패키지 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Semiconductor Package Having a Joint Structure Buffering Thermo-Mechanical Stress between a Transferred Fine-Wiring Structure and a System-Mounting Board, and Method of Manufacturing the Same

**Public scope**

- Replaces the stress-buffering function lost when the organic package substrate is removed.
- Includes low-modulus buffer layers, compliant interconnects and peripheral stress-distribution regions.

## D2. Detectable transferred-body structural fingerprints

**Korean title**  
전사된 본체임을 나타내는 검출 가능한 구조지문을 갖는 미세배선 구조체 및 그 반도체 패키지

**English title**  
Fine-Wiring Structure Having Detectable Structural Fingerprints Indicating a Transferred Body, and Semiconductor Package Having the Same

- Supports objective distinction from wiring directly built on a motherboard.

# E. Thermal Backbone and Vertical Interconnection

## E1. Planar insulating reference surface on a rough thermal support

**Korean title**  
거칠거나 다공성인 고열전도 지지체 표면을 봉공 및 절연 과형성 후 평탄화하여 미세 재배선용 평탄 절연 기준면으로 전환하는 구조 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Structure and Method for Converting a Rough or Porous High-Thermal-Conductivity Support Surface into a Planar Insulating Reference Surface for Fine Redistribution Wiring by Pore Sealing, Over-Deposition of an Insulating Layer, and Planarization

**Public scope**

- Seals pores and roughness in a high-thermal-conductivity support.
- Over-deposits an insulating layer and planarizes it by lapping, polishing or chemical-mechanical polishing.
- Creates a planar insulating reference surface for direct RDL formation or RDL-overlay transfer.
- Enables electrically conductive or semiconductive thermal materials to function as wiring-compatible system backbones.

---

## E2. Insulated through-electrode through a conductive cooling backbone

**Korean title**  
냉각채널을 구비한 전기 도전성 고열전도 지지체를 절연 라이너와 함께 관통하는 관통 전극 구조 및 그 제조방법

**English title**  
Through-Via Structure Penetrating an Electrically Conductive, High-Thermal-Conductivity Support Having a Cooling Channel with an Insulating Liner, and Method of Manufacturing the Same

**Public scope**

- A via conductor passes through a conductive or semiconductive thermal support.
- An insulating liner electrically isolates the via from the support body.
- The through-electrode avoids the cooling channel or is hermetically isolated from coolant.
- The conductive body can serve as a ground reference or electromagnetic shield for selected signal vias.

---

## E3. Surface-bonded-carbon reaction-bonded silicon carbide

**Korean title**  
표면 결합 탄소를 이용한 반응결합 탄화규소 소결체, 피복 탄화규소 분말 및 이들의 제조방법

**English title**  
Reaction-Bonded Silicon Carbide Body Using Surface-Bonded Carbon, Coated Silicon Carbide Powder, and Methods for Manufacturing the Same

**Public scope**

- Carbon precursor is fixed on silicon-carbide particle surfaces rather than added as separate particulate carbon.
- Secondary silicon-carbide neck formation and free-silicon microstructure are controlled at the particle-surface level.
- Supports high-purity, thick and complex reaction-bonded silicon-carbide structures.
- Provides an enabling material route for high-conductivity, high-stiffness backbones and semiconductor-equipment components.
