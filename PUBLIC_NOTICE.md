## Cools Package-Substrate-Less SystemBoard

This repository publicly identifies a Cools system architecture involving separately fabricated and transferred high-density redistribution wiring, coarse-pitch system boards, package-substrate removal, known-good redistribution tiles, thermo-mechanical stress buffering, reusable manufacturing carriers, thermally active backbones, insulated vertical interconnection and electrical–optical redistribution.

The repository is intended to establish a searchable public technical and commercial record and to support discussions with potential industrial partners.
